# Can Scientific Research Really Avoid Metaphysics?

Since the last century, the physics community has been unsparing in its criticism of philosophical reflection. Philosophical reflections on the nature of space and time that lack mathematical formalization have all been dismissed as metaphysics.

But can contemporary physics research truly escape metaphysics? Is anything unobservable simply not to be discussed?

The mainstream theories in contemporary physics: the mathematical modeling of relativity takes place in 4-dimensional spacetime, in which time is also geometrized in the Riemannian sense; the mathematical modeling of quantum field theory takes place in an infinite-dimensional Hilbert state space, and also lacks 3D intuitiveness.

But human intuitive perception of the universe is 3D—that is to say, the mainstream theories in contemporary physics are in fact all detached from cognitive intuition and experimental observation, and these mathematical modelings built in 4-dimensional or higher-dimensional abstract spaces are in fact difficult to map back faithfully onto 3D space. Relativity has the ADM 3+1 decomposition formalism; from this perspective, relativity is more physical than quantum field theory, but it is also an observer's perspective, not the perspective of the universe as it is in itself, and thus does not fully escape the metaphysical domain. So contemporary physics is in fact also full of speculative imagination in the form of mathematical modeling detached from observation.

Therefore, these mathematical modelings of physical theories that cannot be faithfully reduced to 3D spatial evolutionary processes are in fact still metaphysics—not directly based on observation and intuitive perception, nor capable of being faithfully reduced to a mathematical representation in 3D space; it is merely that this metaphysics is packaged in mathematical form. But a metaphysics packaged in an elegant mathematical form is still metaphysics.

I personally believe that scientific research in fact cannot escape metaphysics, because an intelligent civilization like human civilization will inevitably proceed from observed phenomena, through logical reasoning, to infer what the world might be like at scales we cannot see; and although these logical inferences are not necessarily correct, they are a necessary step in the continuous deepening of humanity's understanding of the universe. Once this step is abandoned, one can only lose one's way within a one-sided and narrow circle.
